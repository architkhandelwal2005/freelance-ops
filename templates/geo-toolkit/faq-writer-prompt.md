# FAQ drafting prompt

Use only the approved site pages and claims supplied in Claude's job file. For each proposed question, provide the exact source URL and the supporting passage. Write a plain answer in the client's voice. Mark an unsupported or ambiguous answer `[TO CONFIRM]`; do not invent policies, guarantees or results. Avoid medical, legal and financial claims without client-supplied evidence. The FAQ must appear on the page before FAQ schema is added. Claude reviews all client-facing copy.
