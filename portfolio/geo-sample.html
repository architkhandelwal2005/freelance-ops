Fictional sample · GEO audit

Make a brand easier to understand in search.

Nila Studio is a fictional desk-accessory concept. This sample demonstrates the checks and files used in a GEO setup. It is not a client result or a claim of improved ranking.

What the sample page was missing

  • No canonical URL to identify its preferred page address.
  • No structured organisation details to connect the name, website and description.
  • No robots or sitemap file to explain which pages a crawler may visit.
  • No concise on-page answer to “What is Nila Studio?”

What the sample fix adds

A matching canonical URL, organisation JSON-LD, sitemap, robots.txt, llms.txt, and a visible “What we are” answer. The files are checked for consistent names and URLs. Any real visibility change would require a live domain and repeat checks over time.
