# faceless-video

Use with `delivery-standards` for any assigned paid job. Work from Claude's job file, not from assumptions.

## Intake
Require Audience, topic, source facts, length, language, channel specs, visual rights and voice permission. If a field is absent, put the precise question in the job file under `## Questions` for Claude. Do not fill missing client facts from search.

## Method and default stack
Default no-cost stack: Local script, SVG/stock assets with licence log, offline voice and ffmpeg edit.

1. Outline factual beats with source links.
2. Write narration and visual storyboard.
3. Create or select copyright-safe visuals and record licences.
4. Record/render permitted voiceover and assemble edit.
5. Watch full export for pacing, claims, captions and rights.

## Time guide
Small 1 day; medium 2-4 days; large 1+ week after assets. These are internal effort guides, not client commitments.

## QA: pass criteria
- [ ] Video and audio play end-to-end.
- [ ] Facts and visuals have sources/rights.
- [ ] Narration matches captions.
- [ ] No misleading synthetic voice identity.
- [ ] Thumbnail and export meet channel dimensions.

Save the evidence in the job folder; a claim of "tested" without results does not pass. Use [checklist.md](checklist.md) to record the checks.

## Deliverables and handover pack
MP4, script, storyboard, captions, thumbnail, rights log and walkthrough script. Include a plain-language `handover.md` with run/update steps, files, limits, backup location and a 60-second walkthrough script.

## Escalate to Claude when
Voice cloning, disputed rights, regulated claims, paid stock or publishing. Record the question in the job file and stop the dependent action.

## Never
Never contact anyone or send client-facing work directly; Claude reviews and delivers. Never spend money or sign up as Archit; use no-cost tools first. Never invent clients, claims, results or experience; mark unknowns [TO CONFIRM]. Describe Archit as building with AI tools and checking every change by hand, never as hand-coding. Never confirm price, deadline or scope. Back up sites and work only on staging without written live-production go-ahead. Keep client data private; public samples use fictional brands. Do not put “free” in client-facing copy. Do not make unsupported medical, legal or financial claims.

For a fictional test brief, use [prompt.md](prompt.md).
