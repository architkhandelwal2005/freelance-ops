# content-writing

Use with `delivery-standards` for any assigned paid job. Work from Claude's job file, not from assumptions.

## Intake
Require Audience, channel, objective, voice, claims/source pack, word count and approval route. If a field is absent, put the precise question in the job file under `## Questions` for Claude. Do not fill missing client facts from search.

## Method and default stack
Default no-cost stack: Markdown or shared document; local spell check and source register.

1. Extract source facts, audience pain and intended action.
2. Outline with one purpose per section.
3. Draft specific Indian English when relevant; mark unknown facts.
4. Check claims, plagiarism risk, readability and channel limits.
5. Deliver copy variants with approval notes.

## Time guide
Small 1-3 h; medium 0.5-1 day; large 2-4 days after research. These are internal effort guides, not client commitments.

## QA: pass criteria
- [ ] Every material fact is sourced or marked [TO CONFIRM].
- [ ] No prohibited claim or fabricated quote.
- [ ] Clear audience and action.
- [ ] Spelling and channel length pass.
- [ ] No word free in client copy.

Save the evidence in the job folder; a claim of "tested" without results does not pass. Use [checklist.md](checklist.md) to record the checks.

## Deliverables and handover pack
Editable copy, headline variants, fact/source log, review notes and walkthrough script. Include a plain-language `handover.md` with run/update steps, files, limits, backup location and a 60-second walkthrough script.

## Escalate to Claude when
Unverified claims, regulated statements, voice mismatch or new channels/scope. Record the question in the job file and stop the dependent action.

## Never
Never contact anyone or send client-facing work directly; Claude reviews and delivers. Never spend money or sign up as Archit; use no-cost tools first. Never invent clients, claims, results or experience; mark unknowns [TO CONFIRM]. Describe Archit as building with AI tools and checking every change by hand, never as hand-coding. Never confirm price, deadline or scope. Back up sites and work only on staging without written live-production go-ahead. Keep client data private; public samples use fictional brands. Do not put “free” in client-facing copy. Do not make unsupported medical, legal or financial claims.

For a fictional test brief, use [prompt.md](prompt.md).
