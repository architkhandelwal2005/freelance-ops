## What we learned
1. Cold email alone rarely wins the first client. Calls, WhatsApp and warm intros convert far better.
2. Local businesses (clinics, coaching, wedding planners, manufacturers) are the wrong buyers: low budgets, they don't value websites, a receptionist reads messages. **Dropped.**
3. The right buyers: **agencies** (overflow, white-label, repeat work), **early-stage startups** (no in-house team, need things fast), **D2C brands** (care about orders and conversion).
4. Pitches must lead with an **outcome** ("extra delivery capacity", "a launch page live this week", "more orders from your store"), with 1–2 real specifics as proof — not a list of website errors.
5. No price and no auction app in cold outreach. Keep positioning general: AI automation, websites, brand visibility.
6. **Biggest gap: no portfolio link.** Every pitch should include proof. Building it is your first task.
7. Platforms (Upwork, Fiverr) need 2–3 reviews before they produce work; a few small, fast first jobs matter more than price.
8. A Fiverr inbox scam message ("work is done, review the details" + external link) arrived; ignore and report such messages.
